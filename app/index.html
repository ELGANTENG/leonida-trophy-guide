    Five stages, in order. Finish one before the next and the platinum lands on its own — PowerPyx puts the whole journey at about 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10. One playthrough is enough, and nothing but the first meditation can be missed.

      One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude

      GTA 6 has no mission replay. The first meditation — Lucia on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth — is gone once Chapter 1 ends. Whether that session is required for the trophy is still unconfirmed, so do it anyway: talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony, and choose “Accept” when she offers to meditate.

      Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Everything else — including the whole 100% completion checklist — can be finished in free roam after the credits.

      Missable trophies

        Safe either way

        PowerPyx and GTABase both report that nothing on the 100% completion checklist is missable: the checklist only asks for 15 of the 18 meditations, so the platinum stays reachable even if the Chapter 1 session is skipped — but only the trophy itself is at risk.

        Where the facts come from

        Every trophy’s name, description and grade are the official PlayStation list, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles before this guide was built. Every guide is written for this entry, in our own words — never copied from another guide. Each trophy’s detail names the sources its facts came from; a step with no outside source says so, and cites the official description instead. Figures that were still being confirmed at launch (the four vague hidden trophies) are marked unconfirmed in their guides.

          About this guide

          Leonida Trophy Guide is a static web app: it runs entirely from its own files, makes no network request, and saves your checklist in your browser (localStorage). Export and import move your progress as a JSON file. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or fonts are used; the look is our own Vice City sunset.
